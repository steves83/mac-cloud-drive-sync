Mac Cloud Drive Sync

A free personal Mac setup that uses the open-source rclone program to synchronize ordinary files between iCloud Drive and Google Drive.

The program runs on its owner’s Mac. It is not a hosted file-storage service, and this website does not receive documents or Google login tokens.

The Google Drive permission is used to list, read, create, update, rename, and delete files as part of two-way synchronization. Conflicting versions and recovery copies are retained.
