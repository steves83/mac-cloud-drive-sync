Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026.

Mac Cloud Drive Sync is a personal synchronization setup running locally on its owner’s Mac. It uses rclone to access Google Drive with the account owner’s authorization and Apple’s existing iCloud Drive integration for the iCloud side.

Data access and use

The program accesses file names, folder structure, file metadata, and file contents solely to synchronize the selected folders and preserve recovery copies. It can create, update, rename, and delete files to reflect changes made by the account owner.

Storage and sharing

Files are copied between the owner’s configured Google Drive and iCloud Drive locations through the owner’s Mac. Local logs, synchronization state, OAuth credentials, and local recovery copies are stored on that Mac. Google-side recovery copies remain in the owner’s Google Drive. Data is not sent to this website, sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Apple, Google, and any separate account integrations remain subject to their own policies.

Control and retention

The owner can pause syncing, remove its macOS background job, and revoke the app’s access in their Google Account. Logs and recovery copies can be reviewed and removed by the owner. This website uses no custom analytics, forms, or advertising; its hosting provider may maintain standard delivery logs.

Google API policy

The setup’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

For questions about this personal setup, use the developer contact listed on its Google consent screen or the source repository.
